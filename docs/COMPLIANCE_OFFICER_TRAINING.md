# Compliance Officer Onboarding & Training Manual

Welcome to the Stellar-Guard platform training program. This manual prepares compliance analysts, risk managers, and operations personnel to effectively manage asset protection on Stellar.

---

## Module 1: Understanding Regulated Assets on Stellar
- **Clawback vs Freezing:** Difference between seizing illicit balances (Clawback) vs temporarily isolating trustlines (Quarantine).
- **The Dual-Control Rule:** Why every critical action requires two independent signers and how it protects both the institution and customer assets.

---

## Module 2: The Operator Interface
- **Dashboard Overview:** Metric cards (Quarantined Assets, Active Votes, 24h Monitored Volume).
- **Freighter Wallet Security:** Never share seed phrases, verify transaction details on screen before signing.
- **Search & Filtering:** How to filter proposals by asset code (`USDC`, `EURC`), status (`PENDING`, `EXECUTED`), and incident category.

---

## Module 3: Handling Emergency Incidents
1. Identify high-risk alert in the live event feed.
2. Verify counterparty on Stellar Expert and internal AML tool.
3. Open **Emergency Freeze** drawer, paste address, and describe legal reason.
4. Reach out to peer trustee for secondary signature.
5. Export PDF audit record for regulatory reporting (FinCEN, OFAC, BaFin).
