# Stellar-Guard Protocol Governance Charter

*Adopted by the Governance Council — October 2026*

---

## Article I: Mandate and Core Principles

The Stellar-Guard Protocol operates under three non-negotiable principles:
1. **Asset Protection Without Despotism:** No single administrator or executive officer may unilaterally freeze, confiscate, or alter the disposition of user funds.
2. **Statutory Integrity:** The protocol must provide financial institutions with legally verifiable mechanisms to comply with court orders and AML mandates.
3. **Transparency & Auditability:** Every state transition must be verifiable on the public Stellar ledger accompanied by cryptographic evidence references.

---

## Article II: Quorum Thresholds & Voting Weights

- **Standard Quorum:** An affirmative vote of at least **66%** of authorized administrators is required for any asset quarantine or unfreeze proposal.
- **Admin Set Modification:** Adding or removing an administrator key requires an affirmative vote of at least **80%** (Super-Quorum).
- **Emergency Circuit Breaker:** Any single administrator may trigger the emergency pause for up to **48 hours** upon detecting an active exploit, during which only admin replacement and unfreeze actions may proceed.
- **Smart Contract Upgrades:** Upgrading contract WASM bytecode requires **100% Unanimous Consent** of the active governance council followed by a mandatory 72-hour timelock delay.
